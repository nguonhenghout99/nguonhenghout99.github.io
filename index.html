About me

I am a Master's student in Computer Engineering at Ajou University (Suwon, Korea), working in the NiSE Lab under Prof. Seok-Won Lee, and a recipient of the 2025 Global Korea Scholarship (GKS-G).

Before graduate school I spent two and a half years at Cellcard (CamGSM Plc.) in Phnom Penh, building Java / Spring Boot REST APIs, Kafka producers and consumers, and USSD, IVR and outbound-dialing call flows on a telecom fronting-channel platform. That industry experience now shapes my research: how AI, and large language models in particular, can be engineered, reasoned about and trusted inside real software systems.

Research interests

Requirements engineeringEliciting, analysing and validating software requirements with AI and ontology-based methods.
Natural language processingLanguage models for understanding requirements documents, technical text and domain knowledge.
Neurosymbolic reasoningCombining symbolic rules and knowledge with neural models for more reliable reasoning.
AI software systemsEngineering dependable software systems built around LLMs and other AI components.
